Brewing Protocols

Personal reference. Doctors can stop reading here, the clinical info is in the table above.

Morning cold brew (final)

  • 64 oz wide-mouth mason jar with 100-micron stainless mesh basket.
  • 3-4 Harney sachets + 1-2 tsp Tiesta loose leaf.
  • Room-temp filtered water, 24 h in fridge.
  • No cardamom (won't extract cold). If wanted: hot-bloom 4 oz at 200°F for 5 min, add to jar.

Morning hot brew (interim)

  • Breville at 200°F, 4-5 min steep.
  • Pour into 2 L metal flask filled with ice; sip cold throughout the day.
  • Cardamom: 2-3 lightly cracked pods works with spicy / warm blends, skip with hibiscus-heavy fruit blends.

Nighttime hot brew

  • Breville at 200°F, 7 min, covered.
  • Catnip, linden, cardamom, calendula, chamomile, hibiscus, passion flower, lemongrass.
  • Pour into 2x 16 oz mason jars, cool on counter 60-90 min, then refrigerate. Drink chilled.
  • Brewed in the morning so Jar 1 is fully chilled by evening; Jar 2 is for the next night.

Why morning is 4-5 min and nighttime is 7 min

  • Morning ceiling = 5 min: hibiscus over-extracts (tart) and decaf-black tannins go astringent past 5 min.
  • Nighttime floor = 7 min: crushed cardamom needs 6-8 min and the flower / leaf herbs release compounds slowly. No tannin-heavy black leaf to over-extract.

Caffeine rules

  • Hard cutoff 4pm (2am bedtime, 10 h clearance).
  • Don't stack two decaf blacks in the same 2 L cold-brew jar; caffeine accumulates over a day-long sipping pattern.
  • Mexican Horchata oolong cut from rotation (80-120 mg / sachet incompatible with the 2 L sipping window).

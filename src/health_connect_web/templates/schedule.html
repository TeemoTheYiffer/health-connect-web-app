Supplement Schedule

Last updated: 2026-05-10. Context: cutting phase, OMAD 7-9pm, 5x/week training, ZMA phased out and replaced with Zinc Picolinate. Chronotype: wake ~10am, sleep ~2am.

Core Design Principles

  1. Zinc / copper coupling. 30 mg Zinc Picolinate (AM) plus 15 mg from Prostate Essentials Plus (OMAD) deliberately balanced against 4 mg copper from Sodium Copper Chlorophyllin. If I drop chlorophyllin, I drop picolinate to 15 mg.
  2. Mineral antagonism is acceptable. I'm over UL on both zinc (45 mg vs 40 mg) and supplemental magnesium (444 mg vs 350 mg). Co-timing minerals to attenuate peaks is desired, not avoided.
  3. AM coffee + non-dairy creamer acts as a nausea buffer. Coffee polyphenols cut zinc absorption ~15-20%, which is acceptable given my over-UL status and provides built-in GI protection.
  4. OMAD is my main absorption window for fat-solubles. Vitamin D, K2, omega-3, curcumin, resveratrol all need dietary fat.
  5. Bedtime is reserved for sleep-supportive items only. MagTech for sedation. No stimulating compounds, no zinc layering.
  6. Stimulating adaptogens (ginseng, cordyceps) clear before bedtime. Given 2am sleep target and ~5-7 h half-lives, late afternoon is the latest safe dosing window.
  7. Caffeine cutoff is 4pm absolute. 10 h of clearance before bedtime protects deep sleep quality, not just sleep onset.

  • Creatine drops to 5 g, any time with water.
  • Cordyceps moves to 4pm with chlorophyllin (not with OMAD), clears bedtime by ~10 h.

Why Cordyceps moves earlier on rest days: original plan had it at 8pm OMAD, but with 2am bedtime that's only ~6 h of clearance, borderline for its ~5-7 h half-life of stimulating compounds. 4pm gives ~10 h of clearance and groups it with the chlorophyllin dose.

Caffeine Rules

Absolute cutoff: 4pm. Reasoning:

  • 2am bedtime, 10 h = 4pm.
  • Caffeine half-life is 3-5 h, but even ~25-50 mg residual at bedtime suppresses deep sleep and REM.
  • Research shows 400 mg caffeine 6 h pre-bed reduces total sleep by ~1 h, even when not subjectively noticed.
  • 10 h buffer protects sleep quality, not just sleep onset.

Daily caffeine ceiling: ~300 mg. AM coffee (~100-150 mg) plus optional 4pm energy drink (~120-150 mg) stays under the FDA 400 mg/day safety threshold.
